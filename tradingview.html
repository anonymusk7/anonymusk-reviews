TradingView review

Disclosure: the TradingView buttons on this page go straight to the official site, with no tracking. If we add an affiliate link later it will be marked, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That doesn't change what we write. No star ratings: we don't publish scores we can't back with testing data. Details and prices change, so check the official site.

TradingView is a charting and social platform covering stocks, forex and crypto. It has a free Basic plan and several paid tiers that raise limits on indicators per chart, alerts, saved layouts and data features. Its Pine Script language lets you write custom indicators and strategies.

Visit TradingView (opens in new tab)

Good for

  • Technical analysis on crypto pairs from many exchanges
  • Setting price/indicator alerts
  • Writing and sharing custom indicators with Pine Script

Skip it if

  • People who just want to check a price (overkill)
  • Brand-new DEX tokens that aren't on supported data feeds yet

Pros

  • Excellent, fast charting UI on web, desktop and mobile
  • Huge library of community indicators and scripts
  • Pine Script for custom indicators/backtests
  • Free plan is usable for casual charting

Cons

  • Free plan has ads and tight limits (indicators per chart, alerts)
  • Paid plans can add up; watch for auto-renewal
  • Community 'ideas' are opinions, not advice, and quality varies

Pricing

Free Basic plan plus paid monthly/annual tiers. Prices and limits change and sales are frequent. Check TradingView's pricing page.

Bottom line

TradingView is a well-known, widely used option in its category. Whether it fits depends on the "good for / skip it if" lists above. Where there is a free tier, try it before you pay for anything.
