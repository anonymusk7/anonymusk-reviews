Hetzner Cloud review

Disclosure: the Hetzner Cloud buttons on this page go straight to the official site, with no tracking. If we add an affiliate link later it will be marked, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That doesn't change what we write. No star ratings: we don't publish scores we can't back with testing data. Details and prices change, so check the official site.

Hetzner is a German hosting company. Its Cloud product offers low-cost virtual servers (VPS) in data centers in Europe, the US and Singapore. Many developers use it for bots, indexers, RPC proxies and small web apps because the price/performance is strong.

Visit Hetzner Cloud (opens in new tab)

Good for

  • Running trading bots, scrapers, indexers or a small backend
  • Developers comfortable with Linux and SSH
  • Cost-sensitive side projects

Skip it if

  • Beginners who want a managed, click-and-go host
  • Crypto mining of any kind. Read the acceptable-use policy before running other blockchain workloads such as nodes

Pros

  • Strong price/performance for the money
  • Simple cloud console, API, snapshots and firewalls
  • Hourly billing with a monthly cap
  • Several regions

Cons

  • Fewer managed services than the big clouds
  • Account verification at signup can be strict
  • Support is helpful but not hand-holding
  • Crypto mining, farming and plotting are prohibited by Hetzner's terms and can get your account locked

Pricing

Pay-as-you-go hourly with a monthly cap; plans vary by CPU/RAM/region. See Hetzner's cloud pricing page for current numbers.

Bottom line

Hetzner Cloud is a well-known, widely used option in its category. Whether it fits depends on the "good for / skip it if" lists above. Where there is a free tier, try it before you pay for anything.
