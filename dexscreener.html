DEX Screener review

Disclosure: the DEX Screener buttons on this page go straight to the official site, with no tracking. If we add an affiliate link later it will be marked, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That doesn't change what we write. No star ratings: we don't publish scores we can't back with testing data. Details and prices change, so check the official site.

DEX Screener shows real-time charts, trades and liquidity for token pairs on decentralized exchanges across many chains. It's the go-to place for watching brand-new launches before they reach centralized listings. It's free to browse and has a free public API with rate limits.

Visit DEX Screener (opens in new tab)

Good for

  • Watching new token launches and trending pairs
  • Checking liquidity, volume, pair age and recent transactions
  • Builders who need free DEX pair data (rate-limited public API)

Skip it if

  • Anyone looking for a safety stamp: being listed is not a quality signal
  • Long-term fundamental research

Pros

  • Free, no account required to browse
  • Covers many chains and DEXs in one interface
  • Fast charts and live trade feed
  • Free public API for pair/token lookups

Cons

  • Lists everything, including scams and rug pulls; you must do your own checks
  • Paid 'boosts'/enhanced listings mean trending ≠ organic
  • Alerts and watchlists are basic compared to dedicated tools

Pricing

Free to use. Projects can pay for enhanced token info and boosts. See DEX Screener's site for current offers.

Bottom line

DEX Screener is a well-known, widely used option in its category. Whether it fits depends on the "good for / skip it if" lists above. Where there is a free tier, try it before you pay for anything.
