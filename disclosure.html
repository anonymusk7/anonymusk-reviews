Affiliate disclosure & review policy

How we write reviews

  • We only describe features and facts that are publicly documented by the provider or widely reported.
  • We don't publish star ratings, scores or "tested" claims we can't back up.
  • Every review lists cons, including past security incidents where relevant.
  • Prices change, so we point you to the official pricing page instead of quoting numbers that go stale.
  • Nothing here is financial, investment or security advice. Do your own research.
